## Headline gaps (read first)

1. **The v2 engine is not wired into the learner experience.** The shipped learner screens are still the older
   36-level demo (`hosted-app/ui/page.tsx`, `ui/components/*`). The v2 modules in `hosted-app/lib/v2/` are verified in
   isolation and on the `/frozen-v2-demo` verification page, not driving the real game. AC-P01, AC-P11, AC-P13, AC-P29
   are therefore not satisfied for what a child actually sees.
2. **AC-P29 currently FAILS on the shipped UI.** A scan of `hosted-app/ui` with the new learner-language gate finds
   `PASS`/`FAIL`/`remediation`/internal identifiers on the public `/…-demo` harness pages and "pass the comprehension
   check" in the main page intro. The harness pages are internal diagnostics but are publicly routable.
3. **Required content does not exist in the repository**: 1,500 canonical passages (the demo file has six, of 71-79 words;
   none is the required 100), QA-approved Best Possible Comprehension texts, approved equivalent readiness forms,
   canonical reference audio. The domain code validates and gates all of these; nothing supplies them yet.
3b. **No ASR/audio capture, no server persistence, no News Reader UI.** Spoken evidence, the progress store and the
   News Reader are modelled and tested as domain logic only (`ProgressStore` is an in-memory transactional model).
4. **Approved Knowledge Map ACs cannot be verified here.** The v2.0 document requires "all applicable approved Knowledge
   Map ACs pass"; the KM acceptance package is not in this repository. Only the KM coordinate semantics
   (15 RS x 10 P, 15 strands) are retained and tested (`world1-framework.test.ts`).
5. **PROVISIONAL_PILOT values are placeholders** (70/30 weights, assessment search parameters, ASR confidence 0.6,
   recency 180 days / 120 sessions, spoken-expression component weights). They are versioned and registered in
   `threshold-lifecycle.ts` but need real calibration.
