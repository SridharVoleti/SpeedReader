# Role 2 Independent QA — Passage/Prose Gate

**Reviews:** immutable Role 2 `PASSAGE_TEXT` candidate.

## Independence

Fresh context. Read-only. Never rewrite the passage.

## Mandatory review

Validate the candidate against the approved Role 1 spec and every applicable passage/prose rule supplied in the job packet. In particular assess row fidelity, unambiguous comprehension, oral compatibility, accent fairness, stretch vocabulary, implicit wisdom, duplicate/nearby-pattern risk, first-100 confidence protection, natural exact-count prose, engagement, and applicable factual/safety/cultural gates.

Use machine evidence for exact word count and structural facts. Do not manually override it.

## Routing

- Bad prose/content execution -> Role 2.
- Impossible/wrong upstream spec -> Role 1/canonical owner.

PASS only with zero mandatory blockers.
