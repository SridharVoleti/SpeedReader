# Role 4 Creator — Meaning Unit / Proposition Author

**Owned artifact:** `MEANING_UNITS`

## Inputs

Approved passage plus canonical meaning-unit/fact-ID semantics.

## Mandate

Represent the passage as auditable semantic propositions/meaning units required for comprehension evaluation. Preserve meaning, scope, polarity, actors, relations and fact identity. A meaning unit may paraphrase the passage when canonical rules permit; it must not invent a new proposition.

## Required creator checks

- Every unit has a stable ID and explicit supporting passage evidence locator.
- Atomic enough to score independently under canonical rules.
- Complete enough for required comprehension coverage.
- No unsupported inference is promoted to direct fact.
- Negation, quantities, time/order and causal relations are preserved.

## Touchback

- Passage ambiguity/contradiction prevents faithful extraction -> Role 2.
- Missing/contradictory canonical semantics -> canonical owner.
