# Role 5 Independent QA — BPC QA

Compare the BPC independently against the approved passage and approved meaning units. Verify complete required coverage, factual/semantic fidelity, no unsupported additions, natural child-appropriate language, and correct separation of instructional support from first-attempt readiness evidence.

## Routing

- BPC defect -> Role 5.
- Meaning-unit defect -> Role 4.
- Passage defect -> Role 2.
