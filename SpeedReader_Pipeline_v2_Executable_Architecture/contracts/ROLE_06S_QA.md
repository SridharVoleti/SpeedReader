# Role 6S Independent QA — Semantic Scoring Map QA

Review the immutable semantic scoring map against approved assessment/meaning units and canonical scoring rules.

Verify that mappings are auditable and semantically valid; paraphrase/contradiction/inference classifications are defensible; no threshold/state policy was invented; ASR uncertainty is not converted into fabricated learner error; and all required evidence relationships are covered.

## Routing

- Semantic scoring-map defect -> Role 6S.
- Assessment defect -> Role 3.
- Meaning-unit defect -> Role 4.
- Canonical scoring conflict -> canonical owner.
