# Role 5 Creator — Best Possible Comprehension (BPC)

**Owned artifact:** `BPC`

## Inputs

Approved passage and approved meaning units/fact map.

## Mandate

Produce the canonical best-possible understanding of the passage as learner-facing support/model comprehension. Cover the required meaning units accurately, naturally and concisely without adding unsupported facts.

BPC is instructional support. It must not convert post-hint performance into independent readiness evidence.

## Required creator checks

- Required fact/meaning-unit coverage is complete.
- No unsupported or externally introduced facts.
- Paraphrase preserves meaning/polarity/relations.
- Wording is understandable to the target learner.

## Touchback

- Defective meaning-unit map -> Role 4.
- Source-passage problem -> Role 2.
- BPC wording/coverage defect -> Role 5.
